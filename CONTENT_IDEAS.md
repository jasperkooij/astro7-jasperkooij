# Content & Site Improvement Ideas

Research based on portfolios of Director-level web developers, tailored to Jasper's positioning.

---

## Positioning

**Current:** H1 "JASPER KOOIJ", H2 "Full Stack Developer | Technology Enthusiast"

**Opportunity:** Lead with the vertical, not the title. Very few developers specialise in travel, hospitality, and tourism with deep booking engine expertise. Suggested hero framing:

> "Web development for tour operators, hotels, and travel brands — specialising in booking engine integrations and performance-first builds."

---

## Priority Content Additions

### 1. Case Studies (highest impact)
3–5 project deep-dives, each structured as:
- **Business problem** — what the client needed to solve
- **Approach** — what was built, stack choices and why
- **Outcome** — measurable results (booking uplift, conversion rates, Core Web Vitals scores)

Name the platforms explicitly — FareHarbor, Ventrata, Roller, Rezdy. Operators search for these specifically and few developers can claim deep expertise with them.

### 2. Testimonials
Named quotes from travel/hospitality clients with their title and company. Target 4–6 quotes. Aim for variety:
- One from a tour operator
- One from a hotel or resort
- One from an attractions/activity venue

Specific outcome quotes are far more persuasive than generic praise:
> "Our direct bookings increased 38% in the first quarter after launch."

### 3. Aggregate Proof Metrics
Without needing to name every client:
- Total booking engine integrations delivered
- Years specialising in travel/hospitality
- Countries/regions served
- Bookings processed through integrations built

### 4. Services Section
Explicitly list what you offer so prospects understand scope without a discovery call:
- Booking engine integration (FareHarbor, Ventrata, Roller, Peek Pro, Rezdy, Galaxy Connect)
- Headless CMS builds (Astro + Builder.io)
- WordPress development (Bricks Builder, Oxygen Builder)
- Performance optimisation & Core Web Vitals
- Technical team leadership & architecture consulting
- A/B testing & conversion optimisation

### 5. Technical Blog Posts (2–3 to start)
Target long-tail searches your ideal clients run:
- "Integrating FareHarbor into a headless Astro site"
- "Why we chose Svelte for a high-traffic tour operator"
- "Core Web Vitals for booking sites: what actually moves the needle"
- "Headless CMS for tour operators: Builder.io vs alternatives"

---

## Secondary Ideas

- **Partner / certified status** — if formal partner relationships exist with FareHarbor, Ventrata, Roller, or Builder.io, display these prominently. Rare and highly credible.
- **"How I work" section** — process, timelines, communication style. Reduces prospect anxiety and self-selects for good-fit clients.
- **Prune the stack list** — senior portfolios actively remove legacy tech. Focus on the last 5–7 years of meaningful work.
- **Industry-specific landing pages** — e.g. `/tour-operators`, `/hotels` — improves SEO and relevance for target clients.
- **Client logos with context** — logos alone are weak; pair each with a brief project descriptor.
